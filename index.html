About

Hello, I'm Rashin.

Psychologist · M.Sc Applied Psychology · UGC NET qualified

Psychologist with a Master’s in Applied Psychology, specialised in Mindfulness-Integrated Cognitive Behavioural Therapy. I have hands-on experience through clinical supervision under Dr. Prajeesh Palanthara in managing cases across the child, adolescent and adult population. I am UGC NET qualified and have presented and published academic papers at conferences. I am committed to promoting mental well-being and to making a meaningful impact as a mental health professional.

My commitment to clients is to provide the highest quality in care, using only evidence-based therapeutic techniques. My theoretical approach is cognitive-behavioral with mindfulness techniques, which means that treatment involves changing thoughts and behaviors to influence and improve how you feel. Numerous clinical research studies have supported the effectiveness of Mindfulness integrated - cognitive-behavioral therapy for the treatment of depression and anxiety disorders.
